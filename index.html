How to Play

Basic game mechanics

Move with the arrow keys or A and D. Press Space to launch the ball. Press Shift for a brief paddle speed boost (7-second cooldown). Keep the ball in play and break every brick to complete a level.

How the shop works

Spend credits on chests in the Bricks and Powerups tabs to unlock items. Equip up to 3 bricks and any number of powerups. Item details and drop chances are shown in the shop.

How choosing powerups works

After each normal level, choose one of the displayed powerups or Extra Life for the next level. Impossible levels let you choose two different powerups before play.

How combos work

Break bricks without the ball touching a paddle to build a combo. Higher combos earn bonus credits at combo milestones.
